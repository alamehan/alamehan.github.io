## about
@en
I'm Raihan — sole product designer for PT Elabram Systems' hiring-solutions ecosystem, where I built the company's design system end-to-end and evolved it into a machine-readable engine for AI-assisted delivery. My practice moved through three phases:

1. **Raw UI** — screen-by-screen manual design: functional, but slow and inconsistency-prone.
2. **Design System** — tokens, components, and variants, all synced to shared foundations.
3. **AI Generates UI** — a machine-readable **JSON design system** driving both Figma generation and frontend code: *the design system becomes the engine, not just the reference.*

Away from the day job I build **single-file tools** — engines, plugins, and pages that fit in one HTML file with zero dependencies. That hobby grew into the **@alamehan product family**: one shared standard, one file and one accent per product, from a knowledge mapper to a project factory to a documentation press.

**Off the record:** my motto is **"Arete"** — excellence as a habit; INTJ, "The Architect": strategic, independent, systems-oriented.

> "I don't just build things that work. I bleed through endless iterations to forge absolute masterpieces."

### The arena
A company-wide AI innovation campaign open to all employees and departments (individuals or teams of up to 4, cross-department teamwork encouraged), designed to surface AI solutions that improve workplace processes, save time, boost efficiency, and reduce repetitive tasks. Total prize pool **IDR 30,000,000**, with 2nd place awarded **IDR 7,000,000** for a *"highly creative and feasible AI solution."* Stages: registration → initial review → shortlisted presentation → final pitching to Top Management → winner announcement.

### The team
Group 6: M. Raihan Rafiiful Allaam with Riyan Trisna Wibowo.

### The idea — DealCraft AI
A guided, Notion-like **legal-negotiation workspace embedded in E-Systems** walking Business Development and Legal through the full partnership workflow: **Business Context → Legal Draft → Internal Review & Risk Scan → Client Feedback → Negotiated Revision → Signed Contract → Deal Closed.**
- AI composes the initial business-context summary from proposals, briefs, commercial points, or meeting results, and enriches client context from permitted internal data.
- Drafts or ingests legal documents — internal templates, old MoUs, reference contracts, or client-provided drafts.
- Scans drafts for gaps and risks: missing scope, differing payment terms, unclear deliverables, overly biased clauses, excessively loose terms.
- Analyzes multi-channel client feedback — manual text, emails/chats, WhatsApp screenshots, PDF/DOC markups, meeting notes — into core intent, impacted clauses, consequences, and recommended actions.
- Generates revised versions along a **strategic-revision slider** (60:40 protective · 50:50 balanced · 40:60 accommodating) with tangible outputs: a new legal version, a side-by-side **Git-like diff view**, an executive impact summary, and ready-to-send client replies.

### Deliverables built
A full self-contained HTML demo prototype — a 12-stage guided wizard, adaptive split-pane layout, two-way editor/preview sync, a zero-disabled interactivity philosophy, collapsible side panels, a technical state machine, a premium visual design system, and reset-to-demo — specified in a complete PRD, plus final presentation decks in English and Indonesian.

### The run
Shortlisted into the **Top 5** → presented before internal judges and Top Management at the Final Presentation Session (21 May 2026) → announced and celebrated as **2nd Winner** at the company-wide event (4 June 2026). The DealCraft UX case was later cited in the internal Sprint Review as a lesson in UX discipline: after ~10 iterations, the flow became properly simple, understandable, and usable.

## data-compression
emoji: 🗜️
name: Sharing Session — "Design as Data Compression"
tagline.en: Humans run on ~1 GB of biological RAM. Design compresses the message to fit.

year: 2025

@en
A company-wide sharing session built around an original metaphor for design's business value:

- Machines and AI may run on 1 TB of RAM, but humans — our clients and users — are equipped with roughly **1 GB of "biological RAM"** for truly focused comprehension.
- Thick reports, wall-of-text emails, and cluttered dashboards are like a giant **600 MB tangled ball** of raw information hurled at the audience. A robot audience would catch it with ease; human audiences overload — exhausted and stressed just guessing at a document's intent before they can even begin thinking about the decision itself. This is **cognitive overload**.
- **The power of design for humans:** good design acts as a data-compression tool, transforming the 600 MB tangle into a tidy "cube" that consumes only 50 MB of the audience's RAM — they instantly understand, and their remaining mental capacity is free to execute the strategy.

> "Our job is not to force audiences to enlarge their brain capacity, but to compress our message — information, design, everything — so it fits in human RAM."
@id
Sesi berbagi se-perusahaan yang dibangun di atas metafora orisinal tentang nilai bisnis desain:

- Mesin dan AI boleh berjalan dengan 1 TB RAM, tapi manusia — klien dan pengguna kita — hanya dibekali sekitar **1 GB "RAM biologis"** untuk pemahaman yang benar-benar fokus.
- Laporan tebal, email tembok teks, dan dashboard berantakan itu seperti **gulungan kusut 600 MB** informasi mentah yang dilempar ke audiens. Audiens robot akan menangkapnya dengan mudah; audiens manusia kelebihan beban — lelah dan stres hanya untuk menebak maksud dokumen, sebelum sempat memikirkan keputusannya sendiri. Inilah **cognitive overload**.
- **Kekuatan desain untuk manusia:** desain yang baik bekerja sebagai alat kompresi data, mengubah kekusutan 600 MB menjadi "kubus" rapi yang hanya memakan 50 MB RAM audiens — mereka langsung paham, dan sisa kapasitas mentalnya bebas untuk mengeksekusi strategi.

> "Tugas kita bukan memaksa audiens memperbesar kapasitas otaknya, melainkan memampatkan pesan kita — informasi, desain, semuanya — agar muat di RAM manusia."

## sprint-review-brain
emoji: 🧠
name: Sprint Review — "How We Built the Brain"
tagline.en: The design system → AI pipeline story, told in five parts.
tagline.id: Kisah design system → pipeline AI, diceritakan dalam lima babak.
year: 2026
tag: talk

@en
An internal sprint-review presentation documenting both delivery progress and the AI-design-system initiative.

### Key progress highlights
Elabram website fully responsive on mobile & tablet across all pages · 4 new service pages shipped (AI Digital Employees, AI Vision Analytics, AI-Augmented IT Operations, Axdif) · Elnex homepage finalized · the AI Motion System built to automate motion assets · DESIGN.md coding-phase standards researched. Summary: *"Strong systems, clear standards, and AI-driven execution."*

### The five-part story
1. **How design work evolved** — Raw UI → Design System → AI Generates UI: "the goal is not just faster design, but faster, *consistent* design."
2. **The new workflow** — the design system becomes the input for AI: Design System → JSON Rulebook → AI + Figma plugin → Generated UI. "The design system becomes the engine, not just the reference."
3. **The hard part — precision** — most AI design tools generate UI that looks good but isn't accurate to the system. Structured JSON plus explicit DESIGN.md rules beat guesswork: "precision needs structure, not just prompts."
4. **One JSON, two outputs** — a single machine-readable design system powers both Figma design and frontend code (achieving this reliably requires a frontier-grade AI model).
5. **Why UX still matters** — AI builds faster, but UX decides whether people understand and actually use the product. "AI builds faster. UX makes it useful, understandable, and worth using." Paired with an engineering note: AI can generate code, but humans still own quality, review, and sustainability.

### How the brain was built
From playful Figma exploration to a repeatable AI workflow: mapping the Figma API → converting the design system into variables and tokens → component + content JSON with rules included → one knowledge base → a custom AI with a dedicated system prompt → generating HTML/UI straight into Figma via script. *"Faster, more consistent, minimal manual work — from playful exploration to a reusable system."*
@id
Presentasi sprint review internal yang mendokumentasikan progres delivery sekaligus inisiatif design system ber-AI.

### Sorotan progres utama
Website Elabram responsif penuh di mobile & tablet untuk semua halaman · 4 halaman layanan baru rilis (AI Digital Employees, AI Vision Analytics, AI-Augmented IT Operations, Axdif) · homepage Elnex final · AI Motion System dibangun untuk mengotomatiskan aset motion · standar coding-phase DESIGN.md diriset. Ringkasannya: *"Sistem kuat, standar jelas, eksekusi digerakkan AI."*

### Kisah lima babak
1. **Bagaimana kerja desain berevolusi** — Raw UI → Design System → AI Generates UI: "tujuannya bukan sekadar desain lebih cepat, tapi desain cepat yang *konsisten*."
2. **Alur kerja baru** — design system menjadi input untuk AI: Design System → JSON Rulebook → AI + plugin Figma → UI hasil generate. "Design system menjadi mesinnya, bukan sekadar referensi."
3. **Bagian sulitnya — presisi** — kebanyakan tool desain AI menghasilkan UI yang terlihat bagus tapi tidak akurat terhadap sistem. JSON terstruktur plus aturan DESIGN.md eksplisit mengalahkan tebak-tebakan: "presisi butuh struktur, bukan sekadar prompt."
4. **Satu JSON, dua keluaran** — satu design system machine-readable menggerakkan desain Figma sekaligus kode frontend (mencapainya secara andal butuh model AI kelas frontier).
5. **Kenapa UX tetap penting** — AI membangun lebih cepat, tapi UX yang menentukan apakah orang paham dan benar-benar memakai produknya. "AI membangun lebih cepat. UX yang membuatnya berguna, dipahami, dan layak dipakai." Ditemani catatan engineering: AI bisa menghasilkan kode, tapi kualitas, review, dan keberlanjutan tetap milik manusia.

### Cara otaknya dibangun
Dari eksplorasi Figma yang main-main ke alur kerja AI yang bisa diulang: memetakan Figma API → mengubah design system jadi variabel dan token → JSON komponen + konten lengkap dengan aturannya → satu knowledge base → AI kustom dengan system prompt khusus → menghasilkan HTML/UI langsung ke Figma lewat script. *"Lebih cepat, lebih konsisten, kerja manual minimal — dari eksplorasi iseng ke sistem yang bisa dipakai ulang."*

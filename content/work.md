@en
A multi-product hiring ecosystem where clarity has to survive dense workflows, approvals, permissions, edge cases, and constant operational change — designed solo since 2023.

### Challenge
Bring coherence to a broad internal platform while continuously shipping new modules, simplifying legacy flows, and keeping business, product, and engineering aligned.

### Approach
Map the real operational journey, prototype the high-risk interactions, centralize every design decision in one documentation sheet, establish reusable patterns, and iterate through stakeholder review and UAT.

Faster approvals with less friction, clearer recruiter focus, unified cross-product behavior, timely candidate feedback via automated surveys — and a design foundation that now supports AI-assisted delivery.

Menghadirkan koherensi di platform internal yang luas sambil terus merilis modul baru, menyederhanakan flow lama, dan menjaga bisnis, produk, serta engineering tetap sejalan.

### Pendekatan
Petakan perjalanan operasional yang sebenarnya, prototipekan interaksi berisiko tinggi, pusatkan semua keputusan desain di satu lembar dokumentasi, bangun pola yang bisa dipakai ulang, lalu iterasi lewat review stakeholder dan UAT.

## design-system
emoji: 🧬
name: AI-ready Design System
tagline.en: One design system. Two synchronized outputs.
tagline.id: Satu design system. Dua keluaran tersinkron.
period: 2023–now
impact.en: 70+ components, machine-readable
impact.id: 70+ komponen, machine-readable
mark: 70+
tag: design system
tag: json
tag: figma plugin
swatch: #7c3aed

@en
Most AI tools can make attractive UI. The harder problem is making every output precisely obey the same components, spacing, variants, icons, and interaction rules.

### Challenge
Move beyond a design library that humans reference manually and create a structured source of truth that machines can reliably interpret.

### Approach
Synchronize foundations and 70+ hand-crafted components (Atomic → Basic → Advanced → Panel tiers, slot-based variants), encode tokens and rules as JSON, document coding constraints in DESIGN.md, and build a Figma-plugin pipeline.

### Outcome
The design system becomes the engine — not just the reference: one machine-readable source informing both generated Figma UI and frontend code, now being implemented with the development team.

### Selected scope
- The New E-Systems design system, V1 → V2 → AI-ready JSON.
- Figma API & Agents workflow; a plugin that generates designs synced to the system.
- Component-architecture practices: instance-first edits, library separation, slot variants — a 100% synced system.
@id
Kebanyakan tool AI bisa bikin UI yang menarik. Masalah yang lebih sulit: membuat setiap keluaran patuh persis pada komponen, spacing, varian, ikon, dan aturan interaksi yang sama.

### Tantangan
Melampaui design library yang cuma dirujuk manual oleh manusia, menuju sumber kebenaran terstruktur yang bisa dibaca mesin dengan andal.

### Pendekatan
Sinkronkan fondasi dan 70+ komponen rakitan tangan (tier Atomic → Basic → Advanced → Panel, varian berbasis slot), enkode token dan aturan sebagai JSON, dokumentasikan batasan koding di DESIGN.md, dan bangun pipeline plugin Figma.

### Hasil
Design system menjadi mesinnya — bukan sekadar referensi: satu sumber machine-readable yang menggerakkan UI Figma hasil generate sekaligus kode frontend, kini diimplementasikan bareng tim development.

### Cakupan pilihan
- Design system The New E-Systems, V1 → V2 → JSON siap-AI.
- Alur kerja Figma API & Agents; plugin yang men-generate desain tersinkron ke sistem.
- Praktik arsitektur komponen: edit instance dulu, pemisahan library, varian slot — sistem 100% sinkron.

@en

Turn a fragmented, expert-heavy process into a guided experience that stays strategically useful to Business Development and Legal teams.

### Approach
A guided, Notion-like workspace embedded in E-Systems: a 12-stage wizard from Business Context → Legal Draft → Review & Risk Scan → Client Feedback → Negotiated Revision → Signed → Closed; multi-channel feedback analysis; a strategic-revision slider (60:40 · 50:50 · 40:60); Git-like diff views; ready-to-send replies.

### Outcome
Shortlisted into the Top 5, pitched to Top Management, and awarded **2nd place** in Elabram's company-wide AIDEA 2026 competition (team of two, prize IDR 7,000,000). Later cited in the internal Sprint Review as a lesson in UX discipline: ~10 iterations until the flow became properly simple.

### Selected scope
- A full self-contained HTML demo with a 12-stage guided wizard and adaptive split panes.
- A complete PRD, a technical state machine, and bilingual final presentation decks.
@id

### Tantangan
Mengubah proses yang terfragmentasi dan sangat bergantung pada ahli menjadi pengalaman terpandu yang tetap berguna secara strategis bagi tim Business Development dan Legal.
